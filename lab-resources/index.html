Object Recognition and Comprehension for Archiving Marine Species

A species-level benchmark that pairs bounding boxes with instance-level, expert-verified captions, so models can be tested on detection, image grounding and captioning of marine life.

ECCV 2026Video Q&A

MarineEVT

Advancing Event-Centric Marine Video Understanding via Visual Tool Reasoning

Underwater video events are sparse, brief and unevenly spread. MarineEVT annotates them as question-answer pairs covering semantic, contextual, spatial, temporal and causal reasoning, alongside the EVT-R1 tool-reasoning baseline.

HKCoral

Benchmark for Dense Coral Growth Form Segmentation in the Wild

Coral images taken in real Hong Kong conditions: low visibility, motion blur, clutter and colour distortion. Every pixel is labelled by coral growth form, which lets models generalise to reefs they have not seen.

CoralSCOP

Segment any COral Image on this Planet

The first foundation model for dense coral reef segmentation. It outlines individual corals at several levels of detail, from coral versus non-coral to growth form and genus, and powers the CoralSCOP-LAT tool below.

CoralSCOP-LAT

Coral labelling and analysis tool

Upload reef photos and CoralSCOP segments every coral automatically. You then label, clean up and measure the results in the browser.

  • Automatic coral masks, with filters for size, confidence and overlap
  • Custom labels with per-image area statistics
  • Reference lines to convert pixels to real-world scale
  • Coral Watch colour chart for bleaching assessment
  • Save and reopen projects as a single file
