About

Sunlit zone ·
0 – 200 m

I am Yiwei Chen, also Jackson, a third-year PhD candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.

I work on computer vision for marine and biology studies: recognition, and the problems that sit between 2D and 3D vision. At HKUST I work closely with Prof. Ziqiang Zheng (UESTC) and am supervised by Prof. Sai-Kit Yeung.

If you would like to talk about the work, or about a collaboration, write to jackson.chen.yiwei@gmail.com or ychenmb@connect.ust.hk.

Seafloor · 4,800 m

© 2026 Yiwei Chen · HKUST

The water is live, and it is all around you: drag to look in any direction (or use the view pad in the corner). The scene also turns slowly on its own: rest the pointer on the right edge to hurry it, or on the left edge to turn it back, or press Explore (E) to clear the page and swim through it. Your pointer carries a torch into the dark; fish part around it and plankton light up. Drag an animal to move it, click one to talk with it, and switch on Vision (the eye up top, or V) to see the sea the way a detector does.

Replies come from free models via LLM7 or Pollinations, then DeepSeek, with offline answers as a last resort.
